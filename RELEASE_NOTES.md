Foreign currencies are now converted at the rate of the day, and imports keep each transaction's real currency.

- **Rate of the day:** Spending and income in another currency count at the European Central Bank reference rate of their booking day. If a file states the exact amount in your currency, that amount is used instead. Account balances use the current rate. The app only downloads the public rate tables; none of your data leaves your Mac.
- **Currencies in files:** Amounts like "-$30.61" or a column such as "Amount (USD)" are imported in that currency instead of the account's. Currency exchanges and top-ups count as transfers, not as spending.
- **Currency check:** If a file's transactions are in another currency than the account, the import preview says so. For a new, empty account it offers to switch the account's currency first.
- **Balance from files imported earlier:** Importing such a file again now shows the preview with its balance and a button to save it, instead of stopping at "File already imported".
