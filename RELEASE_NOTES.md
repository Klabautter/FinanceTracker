Account balances are now picked up from more statements, and you can take just the balance from a file.

- **Balance only:** The import preview shows the balance found in a file and can save only that balance, without importing the file's transactions. Useful when the transactions already come from another export.
- **More statement layouts:** Balances are also read from summary boxes where the label, amount and date are stacked ("Closing balance", amount, "at end of 31 Oct 2026"), with month names in English and German.
- **Other currencies:** A balance stated in another currency than the account's is converted at the current exchange rate and marked as converted.
- **Files imported earlier:** Importing a file again can add its balance even if all its transactions are already in the account.
