Adds a total balance across all accounts, with a per-account split and how long it lasts at your typical spending.

- **Total balance:** The Overview shows the combined cash balance of all accounts in your base currency and how many months it would last without income, based on a typical month of your recent spending. A single unusually expensive month, such as a car purchase, does not distort it. Transfers between your own accounts are not counted as spending; savings plans are.
- **Accounts page:** A new summary at the top shows how the balance is split across accounts. Each account shows its balance and when it was last reported. Accounts is now in the Everyday group of the sidebar, directly below Overview.
- **Real balances:** Bank sync now also fetches each account's booked balance. Imported statements with a balance column set the balance after their latest transaction. An older statement never replaces a newer balance. Accounts without a known balance are listed instead of being counted as zero, and foreign currencies are converted at the reference rate.
- **Undo for suggestions:** Confirming a recurring suggestion can be undone with Cmd-Z, like hiding one.
- Faster loading of account balances for large transaction histories.
