Bank data refreshes on its own, pending card payments resolve correctly, and many smaller calculations were corrected.

- **Automatic bank refresh:** Connected banks are fetched when the app opens, when you return to it and about every six hours while it runs. "Alle aktualisieren" in the overview fetches on demand. A fetch never runs twice at the same time.
- **Pending card payments:** A payment the bank books with a different amount updates the pending row instead of being counted twice. Pending rows the bank no longer reports are marked as cancelled and no longer count; they stay visible and come back if the bank books them later.
- **Set a balance from the account list:** Accounts without a balance get a "Stand festlegen" button. Enter the balance as it is at the end of a day; the list shows exactly that amount and later bookings move it.
- **Accounts outside the total:** A new switch in the account editor keeps an account's balance visible but leaves it out of the total balance and the runway, for example a broker's cash account.
- **Deposits to your broker** are linked with the matching credit on the broker account and count as a transfer, while the category "Geldanlage" stays visible.
- **Review list:** A booking in another currency is only a review item when it does not match its account's currency or no exchange rate is available. A booking in US dollars on a US dollar account is normal.
- **Imports:** Statement tables with a second line under each booking ("Location: ...", card number) no longer report those lines as failed rows. A closing balance without a date uses the end of the statement period. Spreadsheets and PDFs keep working as before.
- **Recurring payments:** Weekly recurring costs need a fixed amount and at least four payments, so weekly grocery shopping is no longer suggested as a fixed cost. A monthly subscription is recognised among same-priced single purchases. Payments you assigned in earlier months no longer mark the current month as paid, the "Fixkosten fällig" notice follows the next real due date, and the dashboard and the recurring screen show the same dates.
- **Budgets and forecast:** The projection counts today, a salary-cycle budget stays open while the salary is late, the header total includes carried-over amounts, and an open fixed cost above the remaining budget counts the uncovered part.
- **Analysis:** "% vs. Vorperiode" compares the running period with the same span before it instead of a full earlier period. Category-filtered totals for split foreign-currency transactions count only the selected part.
- **Accounts:** The account detail shows the same balance as the list and names where it comes from (bank, statement or opening balance).
- **Categories:** Deleting a category also updates budgets, split lines, recurring payments and rules, so later backups restore again. A category that is the only one of a budget cannot be deleted without choosing a replacement.
- **Smaller fixes:** The transaction type menu offers only types that match the sign of the amount. Changing a category no longer overwrites a manual type override. Loading indicators no longer get stuck after a cancelled load, and a failed reload keeps the existing data on screen. Shorter, clearer texts throughout the app.
