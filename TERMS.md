# FinanceTracker Terms of Use

*Deutsche Fassung unten.*

- FinanceTracker is open-source software provided **as is, without warranty**.
  Figures shown in the app (balances, budgets, forecasts) are informational and
  can be incomplete or wrong; check important numbers with your bank.
- The app only **reads** account information. It cannot initiate payments.
- Each installation is run by its user for their **own accounts**, through
  their own Enable Banking application. Using it for other people's accounts
  requires your own agreement with Enable Banking.
- Use of Enable Banking is subject to Enable Banking's terms; use of your bank
  access is subject to your bank's terms.
- The software license of the source code is stated in the repository.
